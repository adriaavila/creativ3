# allok AI-native image-first prototype

Status: unapproved exploration. Question: which opening makes allok's services immediately legible while making the shipped work the proof?

Source: allok-fun/docs/design/README.md, src/lib/brand.ts and the deployed portfolio. The local commercial page and public homepage differ, so no production route is overwritten.

Audience: LATAM business owners, with global visual quality. Spanish customer copy, concise and concrete. No invented prices, awards, clients or quantified results.

Three directions:
- A / Open Systems: black opening, large Geist headline, a stage of source project images. Recommended continuation of the commercial voice.
- B / Proof First: Cloud editorial composition, gallery on the left and services on the right. Deliberate test of the portfolio voice as a commercial opening.
- C / Blue Studio: bold Signal blue poster and one project window. Deliberate departure from restrained color; Adrian decides whether that tradeoff fits allok.

Tokens: Ink #0b0d0e, Cloud #f7f8f8, Signal #315cff. Geist font is copied from the project's existing build. Shared closing sky uses the existing brand color anchors. Neutral logo state; no fake 'live' status.

Interaction: prototype variants use ?variant=A/B/C, left/right arrows, source-image lightboxes, native disclosures and mailto contact. No persistence or backend mutations. On mobile the page scrolls in a canvas above the comparison toolbar; the toolbar never covers content.

Accessibility: 44px controls, visible focus, semantic text, reduced-motion rules, modal focus containment/restoration. See README for checks and limitations.

Don't: robots, stock team photos, invented metrics, cream marketing backgrounds, italic accent words, numbered section labels, monospace marketing labels, pill CTAs, decorative green 'live' indicators, generic purple AI effects. The existing branded sky is permitted at the close.

Image references: source REI and Vistacampo captures. Generated concepts can alter those screens; they are art-direction references, never customer evidence. In the interactive prototype use original captures.

References studied: 14islands (type hierarchy), Exo Ape (image direction and negative space), Brand Appart (clear studio proposition). Copy none of their identity or claims.
